How it works

One agent. Three environment components.

🔬

Structured-knowledge tools

Eight CLI tools that query free public databases — Monarch, OMIM, ClinGen, PubMed. No GPUs required.

🧭

8-phase clinical workflow

Modeled after how a clinical geneticist thinks — from pattern recognition to confidence assessment.

💛

Functional emotion

A reflective pause in Phase 6 that triggers honest self-assessment and reduces prevalence bias.
