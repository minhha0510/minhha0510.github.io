WHY LOOK BACK?

Good questions
often begin with
something noticed.

An effect in a culture dish. A signal in the body. A pattern in patients. What did researchers notice, and what did they make of it?

This atlas follows those starting points through to established medicine classes. It looks at the observation and the human design that followed: new structures, altered activity, better selectivity, and ways to turn a lead into a treatment.

It is part of a broader interest in learning from biological and clinical observations, and in judging the evidence well. These histories invite that investigation; they do not, on their own, measure the success rate of a discovery strategy.

01 / DEFINE

Start with everyday medicines.

The top 150 eligible prescribed medication groups in each of two cohorts: United States, 2024; England, 2025/26. After deduplication, 226 groups map to 115 research classes. Combination products can map to more than one class; supportive sodium chloride and sodium bicarbonate components stay within the macrogol formulation.

02 / RECONSTRUCT

Follow the founding clue.

We trace established class lineages using original experiments, discoverer accounts and historical sources. Each account separates the starting observation, the development steps and the later design work. A class can contain distinct branches.

03 / MAKE VISIBLE

Show what the evidence says.

Histories link claims to sources, identify how much of a source was read, and retain disagreements and gaps. All 115 histories have author source checks. Final independent review covered metadata and numbers for all 115 classes, 12 deeper class targets and source-gap samples in seven classes. It did not independently verify every cited passage.

How to read the classifications +

Nature-inspired biological inspiration / observation-led means a specific observed functional clue opened the traced development path. The clue may come from nature, human biology, patients, or an already working synthetic compound. Knowing that a biological target exists is not sufficient on its own.

Design from scratch requires positive historical evidence that the relevant functional form was rationally designed without such a working precedent. A synthetic manufacturing route does not establish that origin. Planned screening can still be observation-led.

Documented and inferred distinguish direct historical support from a bounded interpretation. Observation-led discovery and serendipity are separate dimensions. Later substantial chemical design stays visible in both.

These are class histories, not individual histories for every later medicine. The cohort is selected, all 115 class records are collected, and shared lineages are not independent discovery events. Prescription versions are included with OTC overlap recorded where assessed; the site is not prescribing guidance.
